**Medium:** a digital storage oscilloscope with a logic analyser under it. The run is the capture; the heads are digital lanes on the same time base; a recorded run is the REF waveform; the panes are the scope's menus (TRAIN, HEADS, FEATURES); RUN/STOP is the key.
**Type:** B612 and B612 Mono, drawn for Airbus cockpit displays: made to be read fast, small and on dark screens. Mono for anything the model writes or the instrument measures; sans for labels.
**Palette:** screen #08090a in a graphite bezel #121314; phosphor #e4e7df at the model's confidence; channels as on a scope: CH1 yellow #f2cf3a (training loss), CH2 cyan #3fcfe0 (held-out), CH3 magenta #df63c4 (learning rate), CH4 blue #5b8def (copy loss); attention and copying orange #ff9b3d; RUN green #3fd07f, STOP red #ff5a47; REF in dim phosphor.
**Motion rule:** only change moves, and motion says what changed: a new glyph strikes bright and decays in 250 ms; the induction moment draws its marker down through trace and lanes once (900 ms). With reduced motion nothing does.
**Refused:** paper and serifs, taglines, CRT glow and scanlines, gradients, glass, neon, rounded cards, icons, decoration of any kind: every mark on the screen is a measurement, a label or a control.
